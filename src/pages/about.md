## The Intersection of Systems, Science, and Craft

I am a San Antonio-based builder, student, and operator driven by a singular focus: understanding how complex systems work—and finding leverage points to optimize them. My background bridges the high-pressure precision of elite hospitality, the rigorous logic of technical science, and the calculated risk of strategic investment.

---

### Professional Craft & Hospitality

With over 12 years in the hospitality industry, I currently manage the craft behind the bar at Tejas Lounge. For me, mixology has never been just about service; it’s an exercise in chemistry, workflow efficiency, and sensory architecture. I specialize in advanced techniques like fat-washing spirits and aging eggnog, applying a methodical, first-principles approach to flavor development and recipe engineering.

### Physics & Engineering

My academic path is rooted in a desire to decode the physical world. I am pursuing a **Bachelor of Arts in Physics** with a minor in **Entrepreneurship**. This structural pivot from strict engineering to a broader physics and business framework allows me to maximize my return on academic investment. I treat the classroom as a laboratory for analytical problem-solving, thriving on the challenge of breaking complex, macro-level systems down into their most fundamental truths.

### Strategy, Real Estate & "The Rotation"

Outside of the classroom and the lounge, I execute on data-driven investment strategies. I utilize **Python** to model market data and manage a portfolio focused on macroeconomic indicators and technology. 

In real estate, my approach is governed by the same systems-based logic. I focus heavily on "house hacking" and targeted wholesale acquisitions in San Antonio's Southside, specifically utilizing a strict "Buy Box" targeting value-add properties built between 1980 and 2010.

Currently, my primary creative focus is **The Rotation**: a long-term project to build a modular brand accelerator and listening bar concept. It is the ultimate convergence of my background—combining curated physical environments, high-level hospitality, and scalable business infrastructure.

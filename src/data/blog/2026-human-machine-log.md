# 2026 Human Machine Log

**Protocol:** Mobility + Hypertrophy + Zone 2 Engine  
**Philosophy:** Compound interest through deliberate, long-horizon programming.  
**Update Cadence:** Every major program block (8–12 weeks)

---

## 1. Nutritional Architecture
**Objective:** High-ROI tracking with minimal friction. Focus on metabolic baselines and reliable system inputs.

### Macro Baselines
- **Target Intake:** Deficitof 200-400 calories. 2,100 - 2,300 kcal / day *(Autoregulated based on daily expenditure)*
- **Protein Floor:** 180g / day *(Non-negotiable requirement)*
- **Hydration:** 2 Liters / day + Sodium/Potassium electrolytes pre-training

### Core System Inputs
*Primary fuel sources optimized for high protein, operational efficiency, and sustainable digestion.*
- **Primary Proteins:** Roasted chicken preparations, lean ground beef, greek yogurt.
- **Complex Carbohydrates & Fiber:** Traditional Middle Eastern staples (lentils, chickpeas, bulgur), jasmine rice, sourdough.
- **Fats:** Olive oil, whole eggs, almonds.
  
### Nutritional Systems Reflection
> **Current Insight:** Be prepared to be a little hungry. Remember, you're not starving. It's okay to be a little hungry after a meal, before sleep, etc. You will have another meal. 
Keeping a relativly healthy diet based on loose fittings of mediterranian diet keeps energy levels in check, fullness, needed fat. Lower carbs are likely so eat them before workouts if you can to keep a high intensity. Salads before meals, eating before going out, skipping nights of drinking, lower calorie drinks like Martinis or light beer if so. Just be conservative in all of your actions when concerning energy intake.

## 2. Current Program Block (Active)

**Block:** Phase 1 — Foundation Building 
**Dates:** May 2026 – Present  
**Focus:** Progressive overload on primary lifts, maintaining hip/shoulder rotational capacity, and building an aerobic base with an anaerobic twist. 

### A. Mobility (Daily Minimums)
*To be treated as skill practice, not just a warm-up. Move through maximum pain-free ranges of motion.*
- **CARS (Controlled Articular Rotations):** Neck, Shoulders, Scapula, Hips (2 sets per joint, slow tension).
- **PAILs/RAILs:** 90/90 Hip Internal/External rotation (2 minute stretch, 15 sec contract, 15 sec sink).
- **Spinal Segmentation:** Cat-Camel emphasizing vertebra-by-vertebra movement.
- **Plyometric associated with the muscle group of the day:** Box jumps, inverted row drops, Horizontal jumps, explosive pushup, jump to soft landings, ski-ers (pause or no pause)

### B. Hypertrophy Split (In-Gym Reference)
*Targeting 2-3 RIR (Reps in Reserve). Focus on mechanical tension over total volume.*

**Upper Body Focus**
1. **Incline Dumbbell Press:** 3 sets x 8-10 reps 
2. **Weighted Pull-ups:** 3 sets x 6-8 reps
3. **Machine Rows (Chest Supported):** 3 sets x 10-12 reps
4. **Lateral Raises:** 4 sets x 15 reps
5. **Tricep Overhead Extensions:** 3 sets x 12-15 reps

**Lower Body Focus**
1. **Barbell Squat (High Bar):** 3 sets x 6-8 reps
2. **Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs):** 3 sets x 8-10 reps
3. **Bulgarian Split Squats:** 3 sets x 10-12 reps (per leg)
4. **Leg Curls:** 3 sets x 12-15 reps
5. **Standing Calf Raises:** 4 sets x 15-20 reps

### C. Energy Systems (Cardio)
- **Zone 2 Foundation:** 3x per week / 45 minutes per session.
  - *Method:* Incline treadmill or stationary bike.
  - *Metric:* Heart rate locked strictly between 130-140 BPM. Must be able to hold a conversation.

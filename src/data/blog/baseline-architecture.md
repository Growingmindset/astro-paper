# Baseline Architecture

**Milestone** Associate of Science and Associate of Arts degrees awarded **December 16, 2023**.

**The Pre-Med Foundation** Heavy background in biological and chemical sciences, including Genetics, Microbiology, and Organic Chemistry I (Grade: A). Human Anatomy and Physiology presented highly complex, layered systems, but the underlying mechanics proved structurally intuitive. This formed the initial systems-thinking substrate.

**The Pivot (Systems vs. High-Friction Care)** The decision to abort the medical trajectory was a calculated pivot based on a first-principles analysis:
- **Asymmetrical Capital Allocation:** Medical school requires massive upfront capital for a delayed payout.
- **High-Friction Behavioral Economics:** Preventive medicine is a failing model; people routinely ignore optimal inputs until physical systems break down.
- **The Emotional Burn Rate:** Acting as primary caregiver during my mother's battle with cancer revealed the immense, unfiltered reality of chronic illness. Absorbing that at scale as a physician is an unsustainable operating model.

This marked the deliberate shift from pure biology into computational and physical systems, backed by earlier structural wins in Programming Fundamentals (C++) and Information Security.

**Current Execution (Spring/Summer 2026)** The current trajectory models an Elon Musk-style framework: extracting the hard physics and economics necessary to build, without getting bogged down by bureaucratic titles.
- **Calculus I:** Conquered (Grade: A).
- **Intro to Engineering:** Dropped. A formal engineering title is unnecessary; the objective is foundational physics and mathematical logic to build energy businesses.
- **The Economics Overlay:** Enrolling in Macro/Microeconomics to fuse physical sciences with market mechanics.

The bedrock is set. The wall of proof continues to be built in real time.

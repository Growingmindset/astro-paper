## 1. The Core Philosophy (First Principles)
*(Prompt: Strip away all the author's stories, filler, and anecdotes. What is the fundamental truth or baseline equation they are arguing? What is the undeniable base reality of this book?)*
This autobiography of (William Robert Jr.) aka Rick Ross showed that through perseverence, you can get IT done, even in the face of turmoil--which is usually when you're an inch away from hitting "gold".  
We get one chance at life, Ross struggled early on because his environment was clustered with bad and he could've been one of the ones to be locked up, but by fortune of luck, he was never seriously caught. He was also lucky because part of his environment consisted of his mother, father which were well educated who were taught by their parents (ricks granparents). Rick Ross was able to intellectually observe his surroundings, perservere, and move forward until he became a success. Some luck, some hard work. I have a feeling even he'd say that.
